# Series Bible

## Purpose
Introduce the complete Mahabharata from the Kuru dynasty to the final journey, with faithful chronology, emotional depth, moral reflection, and age-appropriate treatment of difficult events.

## Scope
Plan for about 200–250 episodes; expand as needed. Never stretch events just to meet a count or rush key events to fit a fixed count.

## Canon and adaptation
Use a consistent main Mahabharata narrative; distinguish later retellings or regional variants when material. Avoid invented facts presented as canonical. Treat violence, loss, and injustice honestly but without graphic imagery.

## Daily format
One main event, 450–650 words as a starting target (adjust for reading time), 8 separate illustrations, one nuanced lesson, two reasoning questions, and a teaser.

## Current continuity
Day 1: Kingdom of Hastinapura, King Shantanu, journey toward river, glimpse of mysterious woman. Do not reveal Ganga's full story, marriage promise, Vasus, or birth of Devavrata in Day 1.
Day 2 onward: progress slowly, one meaningful event at a time.
