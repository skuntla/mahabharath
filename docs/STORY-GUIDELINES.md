# Episode authoring checklist

1. Read series bible, plan, preceding episode, and character profiles.
2. Identify one central event and chronological start/end.
3. Write an engaging 5–7-minute bedtime narrative for a thoughtful five-year-old.
4. Explain unfamiliar names naturally; use dialogue without misrepresenting canonical events.
5. Include one nuanced life lesson, two open-ended questions, and a cliffhanger.
6. Draft eight distinct scene descriptions matched to story beats.
7. Retrieve actual approved character images; verify visual identity and costume before generating.
8. Produce eight separate 3:4 image files. Avoid duplicates/collages.
9. Review for consistency, age appropriateness, and continuity.
10. Commit approved text and images with an episode manifest.
