- Reuse approved image references for recurring characters.
- Character identities must preserve facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, signature jewelry, costume palette, and age progression.
- Scene variety: wide establishing, medium action, close emotion, environment details.
- No graphic wounds, gore, or frightening imagery.
- Verify costume/face continuity before acceptance.
