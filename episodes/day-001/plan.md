# Day 1 Production Plan

**Single event:** Introduce Hastinapura and its kind ruler Shantanu; end at his first sight of a mysterious woman beside the Ganga.

**Cast:** Shantanu (approved master sheet); Ganga (approved master sheet, but not named in the story); unnamed attendants and townspeople (background).

**Canon/continuity:** Do not reveal the woman's identity, a promise, the Vasus, or Devavrata. Descriptive atmosphere and incidental dialogue are literary embellishments, not claims of scripture.

**Approved reference:** `characters/file_00000000133081f583355f4f792cfb0d.png`.

## Eight distinct 3:4 scenes
1. Wide aerial establishing view of Hastinapura in warm morning light; no dominant figure.
2. Shantanu's recognizable regal portrait in a palace garden, kind expression.
3. Shantanu listening to farmers and townspeople in the city courtyard.
4. Shantanu riding through the gates toward a leafy forest path, dynamic wide shot.
5. Forest nature scene: king and horse small in frame, deer and sunlight.
6. Ganga river panorama with glittering water, stones, sunset; Shantanu arriving.
7. Mysterious woman (Ganga reference) alone at riverbank in blue-and-white sari, looking at water.
8. Shantanu and mysterious woman meet eyes across the riverbank, cinematic distant two-shot, no conversation or promise.

**Cover:** King Shantanu with Hastinapura in the background, river hinted at in the distance; no lettering.
