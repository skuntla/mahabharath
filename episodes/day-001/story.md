# Day 1 — The Kingdom of Hastinapura and King Shantanu

Long, long ago, in the beautiful land of Bharata, there stood a magnificent city called **Hastinapura**.

Imagine tall palace towers glowing in the morning sunshine. Imagine busy streets filled with merchants, children laughing in courtyards, horses trotting past colorful markets, and gardens where peacocks spread their brilliant feathers.

A wide river flowed not far from the city, carrying cool water past green forests and quiet fields.

Hastinapura was the home of a great kingdom. And its king was named **Shantanu**.

Now, Agastya, what do you think makes someone a great king? Is it a golden crown? A huge palace? Or an army of strong soldiers?

King Shantanu had all those things. But the people loved him for something more important.

He listened.

When farmers worried that their crops needed water, he heard them. When travelers needed safe roads, he asked his helpers to repair them. And when children waved at his procession, he smiled and waved back.

One morning, Shantanu walked through the palace garden. A gentle breeze rustled the leaves. Beyond the walls, he could hear the city waking up.

His trusted attendants were waiting.

“Your Majesty,” one said, “the horses are ready.”

Shantanu looked toward the distant trees. He had spent many days attending to the needs of the kingdom. Today, he wanted to ride through the forest and enjoy the peaceful world beyond the palace.

“Then let us go,” he said.

Soon, the king rode out through Hastinapura's grand gates.

The busy streets slowly gave way to quiet paths. Tall trees stretched their branches overhead. Sunlight danced on the forest floor. Birds sang from hidden nests.

Shantanu slowed his horse.

He noticed the tiny things around him: a deer drinking from a pool, butterflies floating above wildflowers, and the soft whisper of leaves in the wind.

After some time, a different sound reached his ears.

*Shhhhh... shhhhh...*

It was the sound of flowing water.

The king followed it until the trees opened onto the banks of the mighty **River Ganga**.

The water sparkled like thousands of little stars. Smooth stones rested along the shore, and the evening sky was turning golden.

Shantanu stood quietly, enjoying the view.

Then he noticed something unusual.

A little farther along the riverbank stood a woman he had never seen before.

She wore a beautiful blue-and-white sari that moved gently in the breeze. Her dark hair flowed over her shoulders, and she looked out across the water as if the river were an old friend.

For a moment, the king forgot the sound of the birds and the movement of the trees.

There was something peaceful and mysterious about her.

Who was she? Had she come from a nearby village? Why did she seem so perfectly at home beside the river?

Shantanu did not know.

The woman turned slightly toward him.

Their eyes met.

And that was where a remarkable new chapter in the story of Hastinapura began.

**Tonight's thought:** A great leader is not just powerful. A great leader notices people, listens to them, and cares for the world around them.
